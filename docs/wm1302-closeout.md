# WM1302 v1.1 investigation closeout

Work stopped on 4 November 2026 with the WM1302-SPI-US915 board electrically unmodified. Controlled VHF reception, off-air VHF sensitivity, and MCU observability produced different results.

| Question | What the bench established |
| --- | --- |
| Does VHF RF reach the receiver? | One nearby HackRF in a closed cage sent known payloads. The module recovered four CRC-checked frames on 156.250 MHz and four on 433.920 MHz in the same recording. Synthetic 512-baud paging needed higher drive. Absolute sensitivity and range were not measured. [Captures and controls](dual-band-bench.md) |
| Can an ordinary VHF call be recovered through RFIO? | On 154.785 MHz the independent HackRF decoded P25 Phase 1. In three earlier simultaneous windows it recovered 477, 387 and 648 IMBE frames; both SX lanes recovered zero. Swapping the VHF feeds kept the result with the receiver. Blanking the HackRF samples using one SX transport loss mask still yielded 378 frames. The final P4 header-check build rejected the candidates in two scans where the reference decoded 297 and 63 frames. |
| Does removing the lid help? | With the cage open and the station on 152.600 MHz, the HackRF measured 19.7 dB above adjacent noise with the module lid on and 20.2 dB with it off. Both WM lanes stayed within 0.1 dB of adjacent noise. No repeatable waveform match was established. |
| Is the signal elsewhere in the digital chain? | Paired 4 MS/s snapshots before the IF filter, three opposite LO arrangements, normal and mirrored sidebands, 16-bit sampling, different gain and input modes, two-lane combining and reference-assisted timing searches did not yield a verified VHF field signal. A 920 MHz marker followed the expected raw IF offset on radio B in all three tunings; its source was unidentified. Both lanes independently matched a 935.200 MHz reference waveform in another recording. |
| What can the cores expose? | The 8 KiB AGC and ARB program memories read back identically to the stock images. Both execute custom PIC16-style code. AGC ports 0x21 and 0x23 expose per-radio digital energy. Stock-loop hooks exported the analog indices and a radio-SPI reader returned transceiver registers. During a reference-confirmed call both indices stayed at 13 at every host poll. [MCU results](../research/mcu/README.md) |
| Are the gain writes real? | Stock startup overwrote the early 0x08AC experiment. A later patch verified 0x97 on A and 0x96 on B **after** capture. Neither the change nor lifting the stock analog index limit to accept 14 and 15 improved VHF station contrast. Actual register 0x08B6 read back 0x2D, 0x2E and 0x2F for indices 13, 14 and 15. |
| Can energy be saved alongside I/Q? | Capture source 18 byte 0 tracked A's digital attenuation and remained steady when B alone changed. Two controlled low-energy intervals lasted 2.392 and 2.968 ms at 125 kS/s; I/Q RMS fell during the same samples. The meter update rate is unmeasured. The P4 uses this tap in its live sync scanner. [P4 scanner and digital controls](p25-nano.md) |

## Hardware lead at the stopping point

The back reads 18/2/2021 v1.1. The populated antenna connector is RFIO. RFI is an empty footprint. The photos and Seeed block diagram point to the middle SAW package and the neighboring receiver-side matching network as the area to trace. HDF 6943 markings match a 915 MHz SAW part specified for at least 50 dB rejection from 10 to 800 MHz. This is a component specification; board-level rejection was not measured. [Seeed diagram](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/WM1302_module/diagram.jpg) · [v1.1 FCC photos](https://fccid.io/Z4T-WM1302-C/Internal-Photos/Internal-Photo-5634099.pdf) · [Filter datasheet, page 3](https://pdt.static.globalsources.com/IMAGES/PDT/SPEC/308/K1194783308.pdf#page=3)

The SPI/USB and 915/868 population pattern matches the module label. Their electrical function and that of the nearby unmarked pads remain unverified. A future power-off trace could check the empty RFI center contact to one end of an unpopulated link and the other end to a verified receiver-side filter node, excluding ground. No continuity measurement, bridge, filter removal, alternate antenna feed or post-filter field decode was performed. Off-air recordings and owner board photographs remain local.

The installed P25 application was backed up before changing projects. Its SHA-256 is 73b47a637bf28533784da2ec1e45eff35c157c3aa105378e09babc637c2fcea7. On this Nano the application offset is 0x10000. The SX probe drives GPIO22 low at boot; fit the HaLow card only with an appropriate host application loaded.
